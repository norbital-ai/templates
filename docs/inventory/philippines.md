# Philippines payroll obligation inventory

**Review date:** 26 September 2026. **Payroll periods:** 1 December 2025 onward. **Disposition:** open; no Philippines-wide certification. This is the Philippines obligation register. The IDs below are durable provision references; a row records a requirement or a source disposition, not a claim that its mechanism works for every case. An unreviewed provision, amendment, region, worker class, historical input or saved-output path remains in scope.

## Sealed rule state

`seed/jurisdiction/PH/` now has six active sealed versions covering consecutive intervals from 1 December 2025 and 18 voided historical versions; six of the latter were replaced by the 26 September SSS age correction. The operative boundaries are 1 December 2025, 1 January 2026, 6 January 2026, 7 February 2026, 1 April 2026 and 26 September 2026. The scheme catalogue has SSS, SSS_MPF, SSS_EC, PHIC, HDMF and WTAX in each active version. A structural read found 24 settings and no orphan `settings_id` among 1,244 child rows across all six catalogues. A sealed profile proves only that a dated configuration exists; it does not prove complete legal scope. In particular, the wage-floor selector covers only selected NCR and IV-A establishment classes and NCR domestic work.

**Verified correction PH-SS01:** [RA 11199 s.9(a)](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/20190207-RA-11199-RRD.pdf) and the [SSS implementing rules](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/IRR-RA11199-SS-Act-of-2018_2.pdf) make age relevant to initial compulsory employee coverage. A July 2026 PHP30,000 wage with an empty birth date previously completed with zero SSS/MPF and a warning. All six replacement versions now refuse that payable assessment until the birth date is recorded. `tests/ph-sss-unknown-age.probe.test.ts` reproduced the missing refusal before replacement and passes afterward; 25 focused PH tests pass. This does not establish the other SSS eligibility and historical branches.

## Inclusion and exclusion denominator

The review universe is every obligation affecting employment, payroll calculation, statutory deduction/contribution, reporting, leave, safety, records or separation for an employer with a Philippines worksite from **1 December 2025 onward**, including prior service/pay/contribution facts used in that period. It includes national and local enactments; later amendments and supersession; all regional wage-board areas and tranches; ordinary, domestic, apprentice/learner, child, caregiver, security, construction, maritime/media, part-time, piece-rate, foreign-national and public/GOCC worker branches until a primary source excludes them. Human actions such as registration assistance, permit checks, safety controls and authority filings are recorded as obligations even if they are outside a payroll calculator.

Only a branch proved **fund/provider-side with no employer or wage interface**, a **self-paying individual outside employment**, or a date before the operative period with no historical effect may be excluded from implementation. Each exclusion must retain its issuer instrument and reason. This is a scoped denominator definition, **not a completed denominator**: the DOLE/NWPC, BIR, SSS, PhilHealth, HDMF, Official Gazette/local and sector authority indexes plus their signed instruments have not all been reconciled. Accordingly, neither the current count of known IDs nor the six sealed versions can be used as a percentage-compliance denominator.

### Historical R47 — PH PERA certificate input, annual credit and unused-credit carry

**Confirmed input/policy gap; no accepted-input amount failure is claimed.** PERA has distinct
employer-contribution exemption and employee tax-credit mechanisms. PH-AM03 concerns the former;
the employee's approved PERA Tax Credit Certificate (TCC) reduces tax due, not taxable wages.
[RR 2-2022 s.4](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%202-2022.pdf),
[RMC 139-2020 Q6–7](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%20139-2020%20%281%29.pdf)
and [RMC 94-2026 Q10, employee row](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2094-2026_redacted.pdf)
require the employer's year-end adjustment, refund of excess tax actually withheld/remitted and
carry of credit exceeding annual tax into the next taxable year. The carry requirement already
appears in the 2020 circular; it was not first introduced in August 2026. A refund of prior tax
is distinct from cashing out unused credit. Q6 of the 2026 circular forfeits excess on the specified
self-employed/overseas-Filipino return; it must not override Q10's employee carry branch.

**Current trace and probe.** All six active PH WTAX declarations have no elections and no rule
for PERA credit or rebate consumption. The statutory-status model has prior-employer opening
amounts, generic elections and deductions, but no declared certificate identity, issue/expiry,

rebatable payments; it is not a declaration of an available PERA certificate. The contribution
engine records `rule.rebate` separately from `rule.employee`; a rebate field alone does not
subtract a tax credit. An arbitrary rate override or invented prior tax payment cannot substitute
for the legal input and its history.
