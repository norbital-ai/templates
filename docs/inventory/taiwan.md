# Taiwan payroll obligation inventory

Review in progress: 26 September 2026. Scope: all applicable Taiwan worksites, employer and
worker classes, for payroll from 1 December 2025 onward. Earlier service, insured salary and
tax-year history remain relevant when a December 2025 or later rule reads them. This is Taiwan's
independent jurisdiction register. **Taiwan is not certified.** The rows below are a first source-led
crosswalk, not a reconciled all-provision denominator. “Partial” means a sealed path exists for
some cases; it does not mean that the whole row is verified. `Implemented? No` means at least one
applicable branch or its production evidence is outstanding; the adjacent column names the gap.

## Primary instrument register and amendment boundary

The certification denominator is each operative **provision × effective-date interval ×
entity/worker/transaction exception**, including human actions, filings, receipts and record
retention. An employer-specific exclusion requires its legal basis and entity/worker evidence;
an unfamiliar industry or worker class is **open**, not excluded. Optional government grants
are outside the mandatory-employer denominator unless the employer elects to claim them, in
which case claim conditions and evidence enter scope. No issuer-wide provision count has yet
been reconciled, so the 123 branch rows below are **not** a completion percentage.

Source-index sweep status: the MOL [labor-conditions](https://laws.mol.gov.tw/flaw/index-1.aspx?fcode=A004),
[employment-service](https://laws.mol.gov.tw/FLAW/index-1.aspx?fcode=A008),
[OSH](https://laws.mol.gov.tw/flaw/index-1.aspx?fcode=A005) and
[labor-relations](https://laws.mol.gov.tw/FLAW/index-1.aspx?fcode=A001) indexes were checked
for dated changes, then cross-checked against Gazette orders where the MOL register lagged.
This exposed the operative OSH commencement, July pension expansion and April foreign-worker
fee change below. Full article-by-article reconciliation of these indexes and the BLI, MOF,
NHIA and other issuers' historical tables remains open; the register is not a closed legal
universe.
