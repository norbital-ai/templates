# China payroll obligations — Shanghai and Kunming

Scope: employment and payroll from **1 December 2025 onward**, at an actual Shanghai or Kunming worksite. Review date: 27 September 2026. This is the independent China obligation register. **Certification: open for both cities.** There is no `seed/jurisdiction/CN/` profile or CN production probe. A documented obligation is not evidence that its calculation, remittance or delivery works.

## Review contract

Every ID below needs: (1) controlling national/provincial/city text and amendment history, (2) effective interval, (3) actor and worker classification, (4) required input and its provenance, (5) sealed rule version or an explicit external workflow, (6) independent normal, boundary and exception cases against saved payroll output, and (7) a second legal review. A blank input must refuse a payroll run where it controls a payable; it must not select a convenient default. A newly published rate must be a new dated version, including any retroactive top-up liability. `OPEN` means some of this evidence is missing.

The company needs the worker's **actual worksite district**, employing legal entity, employment/retirement class, written contract and collective-agreement terms, work schedule approval, tax residence, prior service and same-employer reporting history. The current payroll configuration exposes `company.region` and basic employment terms, but has no CN-specific assurance that `region` is the actual worksite district. Neither a citywide Kunming rate nor a gross-wage Shanghai floor check is safe.

### Legal-source denominator and exclusions

The population to certify is **all employer and worker payroll/HR obligations applicable to an employment or covered worker relationship at a Shanghai or Kunming worksite for each date from 1 December 2025**, including conditional construction, platform, dispatch, public-benefit, public-research and post-retirement branches. Amounts paid directly by a public insurance agency to a former worker, and government-only administration, are excluded from the ordinary employer calculation **only after actor and payer are recorded**; any employer contribution, document, claim, top-up or repayment duty returns to scope. Contractual and collective terms can increase statutory minima and must be recorded as inputs. This register is not yet a closed source denominator.
