# Indonesia payroll obligations — working inventory

Reviewed 27 September 2026 for pay periods from **1 December 2025**. This independent Indonesia
register is not a certification. It covers
identified national instruments and local floors, including floors absent from the ID seed; the
province/regency/city and KBLI schedule, amendment history, and several worker classes remain
unreconciled. **No complete provision denominator or independent legal sign-off exists yet.**

## Scope and source ledger

The ID seed has three active sealed periods: 1 December 2025–1 January 2026,
1 January–1 March 2026, and 1 March 2026 onward. The governing row and its dated child rows
are in [`seed/jurisdiction/ID/`](../../seed/jurisdiction/ID/). This does not mean every local
floor or employment class is represented. Its declared regional minimum wages include DKI
Jakarta and West Java locations, but do not provide a complete Indonesian locality register or
KBLI selector. A valid locality/sector that lacks a rate must refuse rather than use a lower
province floor.
