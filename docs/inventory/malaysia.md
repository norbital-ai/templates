# Malaysia payroll obligations — independent inventory

Scope: every applicable Malaysian payroll period from **1 December 2025 onward**, including earlier earnings, contribution, service, tax and employer-registration history needed for a current calculation. Reviewed 27 September 2026. This is the Malaysia closure register for both `MY` and `MY-nihon`; the latter is a Malaysian employment profile, not Japan. The **Implemented?** column answers whether the entire obligation row has been implemented and independently verified, including its date, exception and persistence branches; a partial mechanism still reads **No**. A row is **open** until its operative source and amendments, employer/worker/territory selector, required dated inputs, rule/output, independent boundary probe and saved/settled evidence are reconciled. A sealed snapshot alone does not certify the profile.

The active settings intervals in **each** of `MY` and `MY-nihon` are 1 December 2025, 1 January 2026, 1 June 2026, 8 July 2026, 1 June 2028 and 1 June 2031 through the next interval. Each has 7–8 active statutory schemes: EPF citizen/PR/noncitizen, SOCSO, EIS, PCB, HRDF and SKBBK from June 2026. Existing MY probes include `statutory-golden-my`, `statutory-audit-my`, `statutory-coverage-my`, `statutory-tp1-my`, `statutory-wages-my`, `my-maternity-catalogue`, `my-notice-catalogue`, `my-termination-catalogue`, `my-overtime-cap-catalogue` and saved-input `my-first-schedule-wages`; their passing cases are evidence only for those cases.

**Inclusion and exclusion denominator.** Include every private employment relationship performed in Peninsular Malaysia, Labuan, Sabah or Sarawak from 1 December 2025, subject to each instrument's own geographic and worker-class scope; include foreign nationals, part-time, apprentices, domestic servants, piece/commission workers, and higher earners for the provisions that actually cover them. A First Schedule premium-pay exclusion, a domestic-worker exclusion from minimum wage, an EPF election, and a SOCSO/EIS age/history exception are separate provision tests, not a global exclusion from payroll/HR review. Include employer registration, filing, actual remittance, recordkeeping, notices, permissions and remedies even where a payslip has no monetary line. Classify a genuine [Act 872 gig-service relationship](https://jtksm.mohr.gov.my/en/node/135768) separately before applying employee statutes; do not silently classify it as employment or exclude a disguised employee. Public-service and statutory-body posts, and any legally excluded class, require an instrument-specific applicability finding before they can leave the denominator. The row count is the **known** obligation set, not proof that the source census is exhaustive; the unresolved census work is recorded below.

**Direct sealed-seed probe, 26 September:** JSON/gzip inspection found exactly six nonvoided sealed versions per lineage, all with `jurisdiction_code: "MY"` and a sole minimum-wage region key `Malaysia`. All six pre-June versions link exactly seven statutory schemes; all later versions link exactly eight, adding `SKBBK`. This establishes existing version linkage and confirms the missing Sabah/Sarawak selector; it says nothing about each scheme's legal correctness.
