# VN: HR & Payroll seed compliance audit (2026-10-21)

> owner's mechanism-only test policy; their counts, names and pass lines are historical evidence, not current
> coverage. The jurisdiction seed catalogues this audit reviewed remain the workspace's runtime data.

Audit only. No repository file was edited. Scope: `templates/hr-payroll/seed/jurisdiction/VN/version_*`, `docs/inventory/VN.csv`, engine context in `src/lib/payroll_engine/payslip/context.ts`.

## Summary verdict

The in-force VN seed is version_4 (2026-07-01, open-ended). Its headline statutory numbers match current law: SI/HI/UI/union fee rates and caps on the 2,530,000 reference level, the Decree 293/2025 regional minimum wages, the Law 109/2025 five-rung PIT scale with Resolution 110/2025 deductions, the Decree 253/2026 thresholds, overtime multipliers and caps, the leave entitlements, the 24 November holiday, severance and job-loss formulas, and the final-pay deadlines. The defects are in how the seed applies these numbers. The most serious one is systemic. Every premium rate (overtime, rest-day overtime, holiday work, night work) and leave encashment is priced on `terms.base_salary` alone. Decree 145/2020 art.55(1)(a) and art.67(3) instead require the actual or contract wage, which includes job-related wage allowances (phụ cấp lương). Any employee with a FIXED_ALLOWANCE is therefore underpaid on these lines. Two further gaps: the 2027 Tết and 2 September notices were issued on 10 Oct 2026 but are not seeded, and a second-child twin birth gets 7 months of maternity leave instead of 8. The source registry has three entries. Two of them (congbao, vanban) cover the same thing, and no entry covers the ministries that issue the holiday notices, tax guidance, PDPL rules and union-dues decisions.

Counts: in-force rows checked 41 (35 COMPLIANT, 2 WRONG, 4 MISSING) plus 3 UNVERIFIABLE (operative text not readable; secondary sources agree with the seed) and 1 low observation. Source findings: 1 overlap, 4 uncovered authority areas.
