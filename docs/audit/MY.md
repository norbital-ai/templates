# MY (Malaysia) statutory compliance audit — HR & Payroll template

Audit date: 2026-10-21. Audit only; no repository file was edited. Seed: `templates/hr-payroll/seed/jurisdiction/MY/version_*/`.

## Summary verdict

The in-force version (version_9) is correct on every number I could check against operative primary text. I checked EPF Third Schedule Parts A/C/E/F, the SOCSO/EIS bands and the RM6,000 ceiling, all three phases of the SKBBK (LINDUNG 24 Jam) bands in Act A1788, the PCB 2026 schedule and reliefs, the HRD levy, the RM1,700 minimum wage, and the EA leave, overtime, holiday, notice, termination-benefit and encashment rules. I spot-recomputed table rows and found **no confirmed WRONG value**.

Gaps sit in three places:

- **Sources registry.** Three key authorities are missing: KWSP, HRD Corp and kabinet.gov.my. The PERKESO entry was built from a WAF error page. 10 of 11 entries share the same boilerplate instructions, so the registry cannot show scope or overlap.
- **Scope.** Sabah and Sarawak worksites are refused outright.
- **Smaller seed issues.**
  - Substitute (rest-day) holidays only raise a warning.
  - The HRD education-exemption month window is disputed.
  - A stale tracker claim.
  - A pending RM2,000 minimum wage from June 2027. It is announced, not gazetted.

## Wave 2 (2026-10-22)

### Task 1: statutory charged on the slip that pays it

Law: LHDN MTD Specification 2026, E note 8: "If additional remuneration paid before the monthly remuneration, value of Y1 shall refer to the monthly remuneration which will be paid for that month." The EPF Third Schedule, Act 4 / A1788 and Act 800 tables charge on the wages for the month, so the same month-total rule applies. Nothing in MY law sets a separate bonus table or flat bonus withholding.

PCB already followed the model (`person.normal_pending`, with Y1 and the EPF relief including it). It was checked and left unchanged.

The schemes below are banded, capped or switch rate on the month's ordinary wage. Each now carries `person.normal_pending` ("period.salary_paid || period.salary_run ? 0.0 : terms.monthly_wage") and charges `charge(paid + pending) − charge(pending)`. The printed rule is evaluated twice through one `[…].map(w, …)`.

- When the salary has been paid (pending 0), the charge is the month total, exactly as before.
- When an off-cycle slip comes first, the charge is what its pay adds at the band, rate row and ceiling the salary will reach.
- SOCSO, EIS and SKBBK guard `w <= 0 → 0`, so the RM30 band never charges a zero wage.
