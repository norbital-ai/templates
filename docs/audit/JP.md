# JP statutory compliance audit (HR & Payroll template)

Audit date: 2026-10-21. Audit only: no repository file was changed.
Seed: `templates/hr-payroll/seed/jurisdiction/JP/version_*/`. Tracker: `docs/inventory/JP.csv` (220 rows: 219 CONFIGURED, 1 OUT_OF_SCOPE).

## Summary verdict

The JP lineage is in good shape against current law. The version in force today is `version_6` (2026-10-01 to 2026-11-30). The lineage has no gaps between versions and runs open-ended through `version_16`. The figures I could check against the operative primary source all match it exactly:

- the 47 FY2026 協会けんぽ health rates, plus the nursing care and child support levy rates
- all 47 regional minimum wages for 令和6, 7 and 8, with their effective dates
- the 2026 and 2027 holidays
- the 2026 withholding computation, including the full 乙欄 monthly table (231 rows) and the bonus rate table
- the 令和8年度 employment insurance rates
- the April 2026 car commuting limits
- the annual leave bands and the part-time proportional table
- the overtime, rest-day and night premiums
- the maternity, childcare and care leave limits
- the dismissal-notice and final-wage rules
- the abolition of the short-time wage requirement on 1 October 2026
- the disability employment rate of 2.7%

I found no wrong rate or amount in the payroll calculation. The gaps are all low severity:

- One HR-obligation task is out of date. The customer-harassment and job-seeker sexual-harassment duties became mandatory on 1 October 2026, and the seed still says "once in force".
- The 保険料調整制度 employee-share ratio table is not seeded. The share is a free-form input.
- One future-dated coverage expansion (2029-10) is not modelled.
- Future versions will need FY2027+ rates and holidays once they are published.

The source registry can be reached in full and is official. However, every entry carries the same boilerplate instructions, and the registry has no dedicated entry for data protection (PPC), local resident tax (総務省) or the child support levy rate (こども家庭庁).

**Counts:** COMPLIANT 22 · WRONG 1 · MISSING 3 · UNVERIFIABLE/NOTE 1 · Source findings 3.

## Uncovered areas

These areas are covered only through raw statute text on e-Gov, with no authority source for guidance, rates or forms:

- **Data protection for employee data:** 個人情報保護委員会 (ppc.go.jp, 200) is not registered. The seed models breach reporting from the statute, with a 4-day preliminary report and a 30/60-day final report.
- **Individual resident tax special collection:** 総務省 (soumu.go.jp, 200) and the municipalities are not registered. The amounts come from the municipality's notice, an employee input, so the impact is low.
- **Child support levy rate setting:** こども家庭庁 is not registered. Kyoukaikenpo publishes the resulting rate.
- **Disability employment levy administration:** JEED (jeed.go.jp, 200) is not registered. The rate is on e-Gov and the levy filing is modelled.
- **Foreign workers:** the hire and exit notification comes from MHLW/Hello Work and is covered. Japan has no foreign-worker levy. 出入国在留管理庁 (moj.go.jp/isa, 200) is not needed for payroll.
- **Training or skills levy:** none exists in Japan (not applicable).
