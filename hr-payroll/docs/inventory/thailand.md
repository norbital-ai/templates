# Thailand payroll obligations — independent inventory

Scope: employment and payroll periods from **1 December 2025**, all provinces, relevant districts, sectors, occupations, worker classes and insurance memberships. This is the Thailand denominator for certification, not a claim of certification. Every ID remains open until its enacted instrument and amendment history, required inputs, production output, boundary probes and saved/reversal path have been checked. Source review date: 27 September 2026.

District overrides: **Ko Samui, Surat Thani THB400**; **Mueang Chiang Mai, Chiang Mai THB380**; **Hat Yai, Songkhla THB380**. Nationwide sector overrides: **hotel types 2–4 THB400** and **Service Establishment Act businesses THB400**. The hotel's legal type is determined by the 2023 hotel-type regulation, not by its name, star rating or generic hospitality industry: type 1 supplies only rooms and has at most 50; type 2 supplies only rooms with more than 50, or rooms plus food facilities; type 3 adds either a legally classified service establishment or conference room; type 4 has both. A restaurant, bar or massage label alone does not establish Service Establishment Act status. The Ministry's [Notice 14 explanation, items 7 and 12–13](https://www.mol.go.th/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/07/%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A8-%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%88.%E0%B8%82%E0%B8%B1%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%95%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%B3-%E0%B8%8914-%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%A1.pdf) states these distinctions. Notice 14 cl.19 fixes the daily comparator at the normal day (up to seven hours for specified hazardous work, otherwise up to eight) even if fewer hours are worked.

[Labour Protection Act ss.87–90](https://www.mol.go.th/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/1998/01/labour_protection_2541_new62.pdf) permit location/industry/occupation-specific rates, but the Wage Committee must issue the rate for Gazette publication before it binds employers. A 2026 provincial subcommittee review or proposal is therefore not itself a new local wage floor. The operative locality/sector selectors presently inventoried are Notice 14's province, three district and two sector branches; any later Gazette notice still requires index reconciliation. Section 90 separately requires display of the effective notice at the workplace (TH-WAGE-06).

### s.33 contribution parameters at the December/January boundary

The [2026 wage-base regulation](https://ratchakitcha.soc.go.th/documents/98728.pdf) changes the cap, not the ordinary 5% contribution percentage. The [flood-relief notice](https://ratchakitcha.soc.go.th/documents/100888.pdf) changes both employee and employer percentages to 3% only for registered employers and s.33 insured workers in **Trang, Nakhon Si Thammarat, Narathiwat, Pattani, Phatthalung, Yala, Songkhla, Satun or Surat Thani** for wage months December 2025–May 2026. It was published on 8 January 2026, so December adjustments and refunds need preserved prior assessments. These examples avoid any unresolved fractional-baht rounding question.

| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------: | -------------------------------------: | ----------------------------- |
| December 2025, ordinary s.33                  | THB1,650 / THB15,000 |                           THB750 at 5% | January cap change            |
| December 2025, covered flood locality s.33    | THB1,650 / THB15,000 |                           THB450 at 3% | January cap change            |
| January–May 2026, ordinary s.33               | THB1,650 / THB17,500 |                           THB875 at 5% | May/June flood expiry         |
| January–May 2026, covered flood locality s.33 | THB1,650 / THB17,500 |                           THB525 at 3% | June returns to ordinary rate |

The notice separately states **THB283 per month** for eligible registered s.39 insured members; that is not an employer percentage of salary. The registration locality and class must be recorded rather than inferred solely from a worksite address.

The signed [Labour Protection Act No.7](https://ratchakitcha.soc.go.th/documents/17076199.pdf) also amended current baseline branches that the older English consolidation does not capture: s.17/1 pay in lieu of the missing termination notice is due **on termination day**; s.34 necessary personal-business leave is **at least three working days per year** and s.57/1 pays at most three; s.53 requires equal wages and premium pay for work of equal nature, quality and quantity or equal value regardless of sex; s.70 requires wages/premiums at least monthly and termination amounts within **three days**; s.75 temporary non-force-majeure business suspension pays at least **75%** of prior ordinary wages; s.118 grants **400 days** of final wage at 20+ years (and 300 days for 10–<20 years); s.120 revises relocation notice and adds ss.120/1–120/2 order routes; s.9 includes **15% annual default interest** on specified unpaid items. The Act's old 98-day maternity/45-paid branch was replaced by No.9 from 7 December 2025 for its applicable workers. Each exact amount still needs a TH production probe and current amendment check; cross-employer consent/service and special-worker applicability must be resolved before output.
