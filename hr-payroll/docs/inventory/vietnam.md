# Vietnam payroll and HR obligation register

Scope: work and payroll from **1 December 2025 onward**, all applicable employers,
workers, branches and localities. Review checkpoint: 28 September 2026 (see
“Source and coverage review — 2026-09-28” below). Four VN
settings versions are sealed. **Certification: No.** This register is the Vietnam
workstream's law-to-mechanism contract, including duties performed outside payroll.
Its rows are the provisions identified so far, **not a closed count of all law**.

Each row answers the requested two questions explicitly. A tested narrow calculation
does not make an entire provision “Yes” when eligibility, locality, history, saved
input/output or an operative amendment remains unproved. Source abbreviations link
to issuer text below. An external duty remains “No” until the product records its
performance and evidence; no calculation is implied by that status.

## Legal-source denominator

Include every employer or payroll-interface duty applying to a covered Vietnam worksite from
1 December 2025, including conditional worker/industry/locality rules, transition periods,
earlier service and contribution history, employer filings and human HR actions. Exclude a
government-only or worker-only provision only with its specific article, actor and reason
recorded. **No source family below has a closed provision denominator.** The February 2026
Labour Code consolidation is a review index; its amendments take effect on their own dates,
so December 2025 must still use the original text.

## Labour, union and social-insurance enforcement (conditional employer liability)

[D283] was checked against the official 100-page signed PDF and Gazette DOCX. It generally took effect on 10 September 2026 and replaced Decree 12/2022 then, with staged provisions in art.66. Its sanction amounts are **individual amounts unless art.7(1) lists an organization-only exception**; a covered organization generally faces twice the individual amount.
