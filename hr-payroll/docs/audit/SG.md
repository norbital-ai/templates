# SG statutory compliance audit — HR & Payroll template

> **Historical audit (2026-10-21).** The `tests/jurisdiction_*.test.ts` suites cited below were removed under the
> owner's mechanism-only test policy; their counts, names and pass lines are historical evidence, not current
> coverage. The jurisdiction seed catalogues this audit reviewed remain the workspace's runtime data.

Audit date: 2026-10-21. Audit only; no repository file was edited.
Repository: `templates/hr-payroll`; seed `seed/public/jurisdiction/SG/version_*`; tracker `docs/inventory/SG.csv`.

## Summary verdict

The SG seed is in good shape. Every figure I checked against its official source matches the law: the 2026 CPF tables (Tables 1–5), the OW ceiling, the four self-help-group schedules, SDL, the overtime rules, rest-day pay, the 2026 and 2027 holiday calendars, the annual, sick and hospitalisation leave ladders, GPPL, SPL, GPCL, UICL, the EAP from 1 July 2026, the re-employment ages, all PWM floors (seven sectors and occupations, including dated steps), notice pay and the non-resident withholding rates. I found two real compliance gaps. (1) Leave carried forward under EA s.88A(6) is not paid out on exit. The exit encashment uses only the leaving year's pro-rated entitlement and deducts carried-over days the employee took this year, so a Part 4 employee dismissed for something other than misconduct is under-paid against s.88A(8). (2) The LQS work-pass condition is not seeded. Firms that hold work passes must pay locals not covered by a PWM at least the LQS ($1,800 from 1 July 2026). There are also low-severity issues: source hygiene (the instructions are identical boilerplate, and SINDA, ECF, TAFEP and MINDEF have no entry), dead and inconsistent `reference_tables.PWM_ROLE` data in version_6, and a stale tracker row. The 2027 SPR F/G tables cannot be checked yet because CPF has not published them.

Counts (in-force v5 plus future v6 data): **COMPLIANT 41 · WRONG 1 · MISSING 2 · UNVERIFIABLE 2 · LOW / hygiene 6**.

### ENGINE-NEEDED

1. **Part-time exit encashment** (Employment (Part-Time Employees) Regulations reg.7(2), SSO read 2026-10-22). A part-timer “dismissed otherwise than for misconduct” is paid at the hourly gross rate “for any period of that leave not taken”. `ANNUAL_LEAVE_ON_EXIT` excludes `PART_TIME`, and part-time leave is held in full-time-equivalent days. The `ENCASHMENT` line pays a daily gross rate, which for a part-timer under-pays the hours. The exact rule: pay = (untaken part-time days in the leaving year + carried) × full-time daily hours (`employee.facts.full_time_weekly_hours` ÷ working days, else 8) × the hourly gross rate (12 × monthly gross ÷ (52 × `part_time_weekly_hours`)). This needs an hours-unit encashment line, or the leave unit in the encashment rate context.
2. **Part-time LQS** ($10.50/h). Gross monthly wages ÷ hours worked in the month, per MOM. The payslip validation context has `work.days[].worked_hours`, but not a defined gross-for-LQS for hourly-rated part-timers. Add once the part-time gross base is defined.
3. Optional, not a violation: `serviceMonthsAt` could discount employee-requested NPL from continuous service (EA s.88A(4)), which would remove SG-F6’s over-grant.

### ENGINE-NEEDED

- CPF increment on an off-cycle slip before the salary run. For citizens and SPRs, CPF runs `annual_reconciliation` in every month with Additional Wages. `reconcileAdditionalWages` replays the contribution rule with a context that holds only `base`, so a rule cannot read `person.normal_pending` (or any person or period fact), and an assessable "pending" part fails its earlier-month verification. The off-cycle slip therefore charges CPF on the bonus’s own capped base. This equals the true increment whenever the month’s total wages exceed $750 and the shares round evenly (as for every person in the test). It differs for bonuses that fall into the $50 / $500 / $750 graduated bands, and by ±$1 of rounding. Example: salary 5,000 and a $500 bonus give ee 0 / er 85 on the off-cycle slip instead of ee 100 / er 85. The month total is unchanged, because the salary run settles the rest. Fix needed in the engine: pass the scheme's `person` facts into the replay context, or let reconciliation replay a declared incremental rule.
