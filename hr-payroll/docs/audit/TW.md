# TW (Taiwan) statutory compliance audit — HR & Payroll template

> **Historical audit (2026-10-21).** The `tests/jurisdiction_*.test.ts` suites cited below were removed under the
> owner's mechanism-only test policy; their counts, names and pass lines are historical evidence, not current
> coverage. The jurisdiction seed catalogues this audit reviewed remain the workspace's runtime data.

Audit date 2026-10-21. Audit only: no repository file was edited. Seed: `templates/hr-payroll/seed/public/jurisdiction/TW/version_4/`.

## Summary verdict

The TW seed is unusually thorough. Every figure checked against the operative text matched: the 2026 minimum wage, the LI, EI, NHI, pension and occupational-accident rates and grade tables, all 55 occupational-accident industry rates, the 115年度 salary withholding table (17,848 cells reproduced with no mismatch), the bonus threshold, the non-resident rates, the severance tax-free amounts, the 2026 holidays and the leave entitlements. That includes the 14-day 婚假 that took effect 2026-10-01. Two calculations are wrong. First, overtime worked under the §32(4) emergency extension is paid at 1.34/1.67, but §24(1)(3) requires double pay. Second, the wage-arrears levy is rounded per worker, but BLI computes it on the unit total and says in terms that it is not computed per employee. The lineage ends 2026-12-31 with no 2027 version, although the 2027 minimum wage has been decided and the LI rate rises to 13% under the statute. In the sources list, the NHI administration (NHIA) and the Workforce Development Agency (WDA) are missing, and all ten entries share one boilerplate instruction.

Counts: COMPLIANT 38 · WRONG 2 (1 calculation, 1 citation) · MISSING 2 · LINEAGE 1 · SOURCES 2 · UNVERIFIABLE-at-authority 1 (NHI figures confirmed only via secondary sources; nhi.gov.tw returns 403).
