# PH jurisdiction audit: HR & Payroll template

> **Historical audit (2026-10-21).** The `tests/jurisdiction_*.test.ts` suites cited below were removed under the
> owner's mechanism-only test policy; their counts, names and pass lines are historical evidence, not current
> coverage. The jurisdiction seed catalogues this audit reviewed remain the workspace's runtime data.

Audit date: 2026-10-21. Audit only; no repository files were edited.
Repository: `templates/hr-payroll`. Seed: `seed/public/jurisdiction/PH/version_{1,2}`.

## Summary verdict

The PH seed in force (version_2) largely matches current Philippine law on the numbers that drive payroll. These were checked against the official texts: SSS 15% on MSC ₱5,000–35,000 with the MPF split above ₱20,000, EC ₱10/₱30, PhilHealth 5% at a ₱10k floor and ₱100k ceiling, Pag-IBIG 1%/2% and 2% on a ₱10k fund-salary cap, the TRAIN withholding tables (monthly, semi-monthly, annual), the RR 29-2025 de minimis ceilings, every regional and kasambahay minimum wage in the NWPC matrix as of 10 Oct 2026 (NCR-28, IVA-23, ROVII-27 and BARMM-05 included), the Labor Code premiums (OT, night, rest-day and holiday multipliers), the 2026 holiday list, and the statutory leave entitlements.

One confirmed WRONG item: the 2027 holiday list leaves out the four "Additional Special (Non-Working) Days" of Proclamation 1427 s.2026. The seed authority text calls them "special working days", and a repo test locks that in. The cause is the lawphil mirror, which dropped the section "D." heading.

The sources list is thin and partly overlapping. All six URLs are reachable and official. DOLE (the Labor Code issuer and labour advisories), the Official Gazette/PCO (holiday proclamations) and the NPC (data privacy) are not registered as sources. All six entries carry the same boilerplate instructions, so none is scoped to its own area.

Counts: 23 COMPLIANT (3 of them cited without re-reading the operative text: F23, F25, F29), 1 WRONG, 0 MISSING, 4 RISK, 1 UNVERIFIABLE. On sources: 0 unreachable, 0 irrelevant, 1 structural overlap (E-Library is a superset repository) plus instruction-text overlap on all six, and 3 uncovered authority areas.

- Year-end and exit annualisation (RR 2-98 s.2.79(B)(5)(b) as amended by RR 11-2018, Step 4): the deficiency is withheld "from the last payment of compensation for the calendar year". So an off-cycle slip paid before December's salary run withholds on the monthly table; the salary run (or a later slip) annualises. December totals are the same in every order (tested).
- **Open, not changed (owner ruling needed):** RR 11-2018 s.2.79(B)(3) Step 3 fixes the compensation range on the _regular_ compensation alone and taxes the supplementary at that range's rate. The (5)(a) cumulative average method applies when the regular pay is below the taxable range, or when supplementary pay equals or exceeds regular pay, and once applied it stays for the rest of the year.
