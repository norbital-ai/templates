# TH (Thailand) statutory compliance audit — hr-payroll template

Audit date: 2026-10-21. Audit only; no repository file edited.
Seed: `templates/hr-payroll/seed/jurisdiction/TH/version_1..7`. Tracker: `docs/inventory/TH.csv` (143 CONFIGURED, 8 OUT_OF_SCOPE, 0 GAP).

## Summary verdict

The rates and thresholds in force on 2026-10-21 match the operative instruments I read. These are the SSF 5% on a 1,650–17,500 base, EWF 0.25%/0.25% from 1 Oct 2026, every province of Minimum Wage Notification No. 14, the PIT scale, the severance tax exemption, the LPA No. 9 leave quanta, the 2026 Cabinet holiday list, the WCF cap and the s.118 severance bands. The lineage has no hole: version_4 covers today, and versions 5–7 carry the enacted SSO and EWF steps into an open-ended version_7.

The tracker shows zero GAP rows, but I confirmed real gaps:

1. Maternity leave is metered in working days. The law counts it in calendar days, holidays included (s.41 para 3).
2. No public holiday exists after 2026-12-31 in any version, although versions 4–7 run from 2026 to open-ended.
3. The paid s.52 leave for employees under 18 is not seeded.

The source registry has 7 entries. It leaves several HR areas with no covering authority: DLPW labour protection, the Wage Committee, the Royal Gazette/Cabinet holiday calendar, PDPC, the Department of Employment, DEP disability fund and SEC/PVD. It also has overlaps, and every entry carries the same boilerplate instructions.

Counts:

- 18 areas COMPLIANT.
- 2 WRONG: 1 substantive, 1 citation.
- 2 MISSING.
- 2 UNVERIFIABLE.
- 4 source-registry findings.
- 1 low-severity maintainability note.

### Task 1 — statutory never deferred

The earlier note that "no TH seed row defers a statutory calculation" was wrong. An off-cycle slip paid before the salary run under-charged PND1 and over-charged SSO, and the salary run evened the month out. Three schemes changed. Each now has a person fact `normal_pending: period.salary_paid || period.salary_run ? 0.0 : terms.monthly_wage`.

| scheme   | why it changed                  | change (CEL)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PND1     | progressive                     | The regular month's share `(T(projection) − withheld)/months left` is charged only when `period.salary_paid \|\| period.salary_run`. The "with this month's actual" ladder reads `base.assessed + person.normal_pending`, so an early slip withholds the tax with its pay less the tax without it. New facts `pending_sso` and `pending_pvd` add the SSO and PVD the pending salary will bear to the remaining-months deductions. This keeps the early slip equal to the late one. Law: Tor Por 4/2528 withholds income paid in addition to the salary as that same difference (already the seeded method). The law has no separate bonus table or flat rate. |
| SSO      | 1,650 floor and monthly ceiling | `c(base + pending) − (pending > 0 ? c(pending) : 0)`, where `c(x) = round(min(max(x, 1650), cap) × 5%)`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| WCF      | 240,000 annual cap              | `assessable.ordinary = min(month + pending, cap left) − min(pending, cap left)`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| PVD, EWF | linear                          | no change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
