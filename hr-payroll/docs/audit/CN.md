# CN statutory compliance audit (HR & Payroll template)

> **Historical audit (2026-10-21).** The `tests/jurisdiction_*.test.ts` suites cited below were removed under the
> owner's mechanism-only test policy; their counts, names and pass lines are historical evidence, not current
> coverage. The jurisdiction seed catalogues this audit reviewed remain the workspace's runtime data.

Audit date: 2026-10-21. Audit only; nothing in the repository was edited.
Scope: `templates/hr-payroll/seed/jurisdiction/CN/version_*`, `seed/overtime_policies.ts` (CN), `docs/inventory/CN.csv`. The seed prices two cities, keyed by `entity.region`: Shanghai (SH) and Kunming/Yunnan (KM).

## Summary verdict

The seed numbers are in good shape. Every figure checked against an official or official-reproduced text matches, for both cities: SH and KM contribution bases, SH medical 9%/2%, KM unemployment 0.7%/0.3%, work-injury base rates, minimum wages, the 2026 holiday arrangement, the IIT table and deductions, severance (N, 2N, 3× cap, 12-year cap), overtime 150/200/300%, the overtime caps, leave entitlements and final-pay timing.

The weak spot is the **sources list, not the numbers**:

- Kunming/Yunnan has no authority source at all. The one KM-related entry, `kunming.cn`, is a commercial news portal.
- `mohrss.gov.cn` serves an anti-bot JavaScript challenge, so an automated fetch cannot read it.
- All nine entries carry the same boilerplate instructions, so no entry is scoped to an area.
- Many KM seed citations rest on secondary hosts (51shebao, WeChat reproductions, county pages, the Yunnan emergency-management "service pack").

Seed findings:

- **One confirmed WRONG.** The SH minimum-wage test counts excluded allowances (meal, travel and housing subsidies), because `payroll.monthly_wage` sums every allowance.
- **One confirmed MISSING.** Untaken annual leave at year end is never paid. Only exit encashment is wired, and the 15-day carry-forward expires unpaid.
- **One MISSING that is not yet due.** There are no 2027 holiday rows. The State Council 2027 notice is not published yet; the 第二条 days are known by law.
- **Three UNVERIFIABLE.** SH 2026 unemployment 0.5%/0.5% (the seed flags it as provisional, and no SH instrument exists). KM major-medical 44.03 (the seed flags it as inferred). KM HPF ceiling 32543.
- **Two minor documentation inconsistencies.**

Counts: Compliant 34, Wrong 1, Missing 2 (one prospective), Unverifiable 4, Doc-only 2. Uncovered source areas: 14 (almost all Kunming/Yunnan).

### Task 2: month totals test

`tests/jurisdiction_cn.test.ts`, under "dependent CN payroll history", is the test 'a bonus off-cycle before, inside or after the regular run settles the same month totals, never deferred'. It replaces the earlier test, which covered only two orders.

- **Setup:** SH k-p1 receives a 60,000 ANNUAL_BONUS with separate taxation elected. KM k-p2 receives a 20,000 BONUS. Both are paid in March 2026, in three orders: off-cycle before the regular run, inside it, and off-cycle after it.

- **Not deferred:** the off-cycle slip paid before the regular run already carries k-p1's IIT of 5,790 (60,000 ÷ 12 → 10% − 210) and k-p2's IIT of 600. k-p2's off-cycle slip after the regular run also carries 600.

- **Result:** passes.
