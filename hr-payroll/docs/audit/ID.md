# ID (Indonesia) — statutory compliance audit, 2026-10-21

> **Historical audit (2026-10-21).** The `tests/jurisdiction_*.test.ts` suites cited below were removed under the
> owner's mechanism-only test policy; their counts, names and pass lines are historical evidence, not current
> coverage. The jurisdiction seed catalogues this audit reviewed remain the workspace's runtime data.

Audit only; no repository file edited. Seed: `templates/hr-payroll/seed/jurisdiction/ID/version_*`.

## Summary verdict

The ID seed for 2026 is detailed and, on every number checked against the official text, correct. No WRONG values were confirmed. I checked contribution rates, the JP ceiling and pension age, the JKK risk classes, the JKP recomposition, the Kesehatan ceiling and floor, all 125 PPh 21 TER brackets in tables A, B and C, PTKP, biaya jabatan, the art.17 rates, the 133 PMK 105/2025 DTP KLU codes, the PP 68/2009 final bands, the 2026 holidays, spot-checked minimum wages, the overtime ladders, leave, severance multipliers, THR and PKWT compensation. Gaps:

- **Biggest seed gap:** the 2027 national holidays are missing, although SKB 1205/3/2 Tahun 2026 was signed on 15 Sep 2026 and the in-force version was sealed later, on 2026-10-20.
- **Sources:** the source list is regionally lopsided. It has 15 entries, 10 of them provincial or district portals covering about 8 of 38 provinces. There is no Jawa Barat, Sumatera Utara or Kepri source, yet the seed configures UMKs for all 38 provinces, Jawa Barat's Bekasi and Karawang included. There is no BPJS Kesehatan source. Every entry carries the same boilerplate instructions, so no entry is tied to an area.
- **Upcoming law:** a new labour law, UU Pelindungan Ketenagakerjaan, passed the DPR on 6 Oct 2026. It is not yet in force (1-year transition), and the tracker row describing it is stale.

**Counts:** COMPLIANT 44 · WRONG 0 · MISSING 3 · UNVERIFIABLE 2 · WATCH/INFO 5.

### Task 1: statutory is never deferred

Engine fact: `assessStatutory` skips a scheme when the slip pays no wage toward it and the month has no earlier line. Every ad hoc pay row counts toward `PPH21.ADDITIONAL`/`PPH26` (or the final severance and pension schemes) only. So an off-cycle bonus slip charges no BPJS scheme, and BPJS bases are the contract's monthly rate (JHT, JKK, JKM), capped `month.ordinary` (JP), or capped and floored `month.ordinary` (Kesehatan). None of them sees the bonus, so ordering cannot change them.

| scheme                                                 | verdict   | change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PPH21 (TER rule)                                       | changed   | The TER is a flat rate on the month's whole gross, chosen by bracket, so the order of payments changed the bonus slip. New `person.normal_pending` = `(exit before period) \|\| period.salary_paid \|\| period.salary_run ? 0 : terms.monthly_wage`. Rule: `r = base.ordinary + normal_pending + premium`, `g = r + base.additional`, charge `TER(g)·g − (pending ? TER(r)·r : 0)`. |
| PPH21 (December/exit art.17 reckoning, `person.final`) | changed   | The reckoning is progressive. The annual tax is computed once for `[r + additional, r]` (`map`), and the rule charges `tax[0] − (pending ? tax[1] : charged.year.PPH21)`.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| PPH21 (DAILY rule)                                     | unchanged | The rate is fixed by the daily wage, so the charge is linear in the base.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| PPH21_DTP                                              | unchanged | It reverses the month's `charged.month.PPH21`, so it follows PPH21 slip by slip (PMK 105/2025 art.5(1)).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| PPH26                                                  | unchanged | A flat 20% or treaty rate: linear.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

Law: under PMK 168/2023, PPh 21 is owed on the month's income, and the TER applies to the month's gross. The model withholds the month's increment on the slip that pays it. The salary run then settles the month through the engine's month-to-date logic. Not estimated: the employer premiums (JKK, JKM, Kesehatan) that the still-pending salary will attract. They enter `r` once charged, so a bonus slip paid first can differ from the exact month increment only where a TER bracket edge falls inside the premium amount (at most 0.5% of the gross). The month total is unaffected.
