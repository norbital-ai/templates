# Statutory implementation register

Historical issues, model coverage and implementation records. Current release requirements are
listed in the [compliance matrix](compliance-matrix.md). A recorded implementation is not a claim
that all employee categories or later legal amendments have been verified.

Status: `implemented` records a change with its primary authority, effective date and an
independent expected-value test; `external` is an HR or Finance procedure recorded in the version's
obligations register, or customer data; `watch` awaits a published future instrument, whose page
the drift automation watches. `open`, `seed` and `partial` are retired (23 September 2026); the
history below keeps the dates at which rows moved.

Implementation references are the authored models, collection transforms, datatype definitions,
expression contexts and statutory fixtures. Operational employee evidence remains in the
configured source bundle.

### 1.2 Expression context, by site

Six sites, one subject each; the roots `person`, `period`, `year`, `scheme`, `produced` and
`limits` carry the same members on every site that has them. `src/lib/expressions/contexts.ts` is
the one statement of what the engine evaluates; [`expression-context.md`](expression-context.md)
is its render (regenerated by `scripts/render-expression-context.ts`, held current by
`tests/expression-context-doc.test.ts`) and the Fields panel shows the same catalogue. A member no
site declares is refused at write; the open prefixes are keys the version declares.

### 1.3 Facts the model does not hold

Held since 2026-09-17: elections under a scheme (`scheme.elections.<key>`, keys the scheme row
declares), the registration day (`scheme.since`), directed instalments (Form CP38), entity facts
(`person.company.facts.<key>`, keys the version declares) and `exit_reason`. Held since
2026-09-18: `terms.pass_type`, `terms.tax_residency` and `terms.notice_days` (declared on the
contract), `employee.disabled`, `children.citizens` and `employee_children.citizenship`, the leave
event a row is taken for (`event.kind`, `event.relationship`, `event.child_citizenship`,
`event.child_age`, `event.date` on `leave_entries`), `employment.absent_days_12m`,
`company.headcount_citizens`, `facts.<CODE>.registered` / `.since_months`, `period.unpaid_days`;
since 2026-09-18 (3) the child's recorded share of a shared parental pool and the days the
mother was employed elsewhere before a confinement (`event.child_shared_weeks`,
`event.prior_employment_days`). Since 2026-09-20, statutory facts also capture the first
contribution liability date, including previous employers, separately from current-employer
registration. SOCSO declares certified invalidity pension receipt independently of general pension
status.
Not held: a
day-count of presence for tax residency (declared instead; since 2026-09-23 an unrecorded
residency refuses in every lineage).

## 4. Implementation history

This history records implementation scope. It does not certify the legal interpretation, fixture
values or deployed release. Current limitations are listed in the [compliance matrix](compliance-matrix.md).

### 20 September 2026

- Taiwan withholding requires declared tax residence independently of citizenship. NHI premiums
  use a dated enrolled-dependant count independently of family records. Required unknowns stop
  calculation, while an explicit zero remains valid. Statutory declarations can align across
  versions and entity lineages only within their payroll jurisdiction.

- Taiwan salary and bonus withholding truncate fractional dollars. The NT$2,000 exemption
  applies after truncation; NT$40,020 is the first whole-dollar salary subject to the 5% method.
  Salary-table cases use declared spouse/dependant counts; absent declarations select 5%.
  Missing or invalid counts and conflicting active declarations stop calculation. The form
  captures the declaration reference and count, and the write-to-payroll case verifies their use.
  The published table’s 10,080 cells and its out-of-range formula are tested independently.

- Monthly contribution schemes assemble monthly wages before selecting floors, ceilings and bands.
  Split deductions and closing adjustments preserve one monthly assessment and one annual history.
  Monthly tax reads monthly insurance; per-period withholding reads the settled deduction.
  MY, MY-nihon, ID and TW monthly schemes declare monthly assessment. SG, VN, MY, MY-nihon, ID and TW salary cases
  reconcile across all semi-monthly cut-offs; MY weekly cases cover four- and five-week months.
  VN's default 13/14 unpaid-working-day threshold reads the complete month across cut-offs.
  Loss of monthly coverage reverses provisional contributions and their wage history. TW bonus tax
  remains per payment; employer levies assemble the whole month, including departed workers.
  Insured days use a separate thirty-day calendar, and registered LI continues after age 65.
  Deferred joiners, mid-month coverage changes, departure bonuses, VN exceptions and weekly
  closing remained open under C08 (closed 2026-09-21 to 2026-09-23).
- Recurring allowances moved to `employment_terms.allowances`; payroll derives their period amounts.
  The former `allowances` and `allowance_entries` collections are superseded.
- One-time earnings and deductions use `adhoc_catalogue` and `adhoc_requests`, including bonuses,
  back pay and eligible separation payments. Catalogue membership determines statutory treatment.
- Catalogue classes define settlement destination, direction, eligibility and contribution membership.
- HR presentation changes covered employment dates, salary units, departure details, payroll labels,
  scoped create forms, hiring and settings-version controls.
- Calculation changes covered leave eligibility dates, part-month availability, roster hours,
  contribution inputs and Philippine six-day proration. Each remains subject to its applicable tests
  and current legal coverage review.
